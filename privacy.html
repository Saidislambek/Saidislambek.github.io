Privacy Policy — whatif-uploader

Last updated: November 7, 2026

whatif-uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "What If Animated Stories" to upload the owner's own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails through the official YouTube Data API.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other people. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the owner's permission. Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and are used only for uploading and editing the owner's videos.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube is used under the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms); Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
